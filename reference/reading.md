# Further reading

Review articles and tutorials on machine-learned interatomic potentials, from
general introductions to foundation models and fine-tuning.

- A. J. Medford, D. S. Sholl. Replacing Quantum Chemistry With Machine-Learned
  Interatomic Potentials: Revolution or Evolution? ACS Cent. Sci. 12, 891-894
  (2026). [doi:10.1021/acscentsci.6c00615](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscentsci.6c00615)
  Short, balanced perspective on the prospects and limits of MLIPs replacing DFT.
- J. Behler. Four Generations of High-Dimensional Neural Network Potentials.
  Chem. Rev. 121, 10037-10072 (2021).
  [doi:10.1021/acs.chemrev.0c00868](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemrev.0c00868)
  Classic taxonomy of neural-network potentials: local, long-range and
  charge-aware generations.
- O. T. Unke, S. Chmiela, H. E. Sauceda, ... K.-R. Müller. Machine Learning
  Force Fields. Chem. Rev. 121, 10142-10186 (2021).
  [doi:10.1021/acs.chemrev.0c01111](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemrev.0c01111)
  Foundational overview of ML force fields, descriptors, kernels and neural
  networks.
- V. L. Deringer, M. A. Caro, G. Csányi. Machine Learning Interatomic
  Potentials as Emerging Tools for Materials Science. Adv. Mater. 31, 1902765
  (2019). [doi:10.1002/adma.201902765](https://doi.org/10.1002/adma.201902765)
  Accessible materials-science introduction to MLIPs and their applications.
- E. C.-Y. Yuan, Y. Liu, J. Chen, ... T. Head-Gordon. Foundation models for
  atomistic simulation of chemistry and materials. Nat. Rev. Chem. 10, 212-230
  (2026). [doi:10.1038/s41570-025-00793-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41570-025-00793-5)
  Universal MLIPs: datasets, architectures, fine-tuning and limitations.
- J. Kim, H. Cho, H. Jeon, J. Jung, S. Han. Reactive Machine Learning
  Interatomic Potentials for Chemistry and Materials Science. Chem. Rev. 126,
  4467-4510 (2026).
  [doi:10.1021/acs.chemrev.5c00728](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemrev.5c00728)
  MLIPs for bond breaking, reactions, barriers and reactive sampling.
- R. Jacobs, D. Morgan, S. Attarian, J. Meng, et al. A practical guide to
  machine learning interatomic potentials – Status and future. Curr. Opin.
  Solid State Mater. Sci. 35, 101214 (2025).
  [doi:10.1016/j.cossms.2025.101214](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cossms.2025.101214)
  Practical guide to choosing, training and validating MLIPs.
- X. Liu, K. Zeng, Z. Luo, Y. Wang, et al. Fine-tuning universal
  machine-learned interatomic potentials: A Tutorial on methods and
  applications. J. Appl. Phys. 139, 041101 (2026).
  [doi:10.1063/5.0299305](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0299305)
  Hands-on tutorial on fine-tuning universal MLIPs for specific systems.
